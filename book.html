Hozirgi oddiy zamon: Bo'lmoq fe'li

Grammar & Vocabulary A1
A GRAMMATIK QOIDA VA MA'NOSI (Grammar Rules & Usage)
'To be' fe'li shaxs, narsa yoki hayvonning kimligi, qandayligi, qayerdaligi yoki yoshi haqida gapirganda ishlatiladi. O'zbek tilida bu '-dir', 'man', 'san', 'miz' qo'shimchalariga to'g'ri keladi.
1. Darak shakli (Affirmative +)
Ega (Subject)To Be shakliQisqartmaMisol (Example)
IamI'mI am a 4th grade student. (Men 4-sinf o'quvchisiman.)
He / She / ItisHe's / She's / It'sHe is clever. / The cat is small.
We / You / TheyareWe're / You're / They'reWe are happy friends. (Biz baxtli do'stlarmiz.)
2. Inkor va So'roq shakli (Negative - & Question ?)
ShaklFormulaMisol (Example)Qisqa javob (Short Answer)
Inkor (-)Subject + am/is/are + notShe is not (isn't) tired.No, she isn't.
So'roq (?)Am/Is/Are + Subject...?Are you ready today?Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
So'roq (?)Is + he/she/it...?Is it a big dog?Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
⚠️
Destination Tip! Esda tuting: am not ning qisqartmasi yo'q (I amn't DEYILMAYDI!), faqat I'm not deb yoziladi. Lekin is not = isn't va are not = aren't bo'ladi!
Ko'rsatkich so'zlar: today (bugun), now (hozir), always (doimo), every day (har kuni).

Ega (Subject)Yordamchi fe'lAsosiy fe'lMisol (Example)
